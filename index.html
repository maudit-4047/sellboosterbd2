Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about AuraFlame™.

Is the flame effect hot or dangerous for children/pets?

Not at all! The flame effect is an optical illusion created by warm LED lighting through microscopic cool ultrasonic water mist. It is 100% cool to the touch and safe around curious children and pets.

Can I use any essential oil with AuraFlame™?

Yes! AuraFlame is crafted from corrosion-resistant food-grade PP polymer. Simply add 2-3 drops of any pure water-soluble essential oil (e.g. lavender, peppermint, eucalyptus) into the water tank.

Does it automatically turn off when water runs out?

Yes, it features a built-in smart water level sensor. When the water level drops below the minimum threshold, it automatically shuts off to protect the ultrasonic atomization plate.
